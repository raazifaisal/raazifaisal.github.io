U3RF-IR01

Information retrieval

One business, a thousand spellings

An entity-resolution system that decides which messy business records describe the same company. Dense and lexical retrieval propose candidates, a gradient-boosted pruner and a two-stage ranker narrow them down, calibration sets the threshold, and a small language model gets the final veto.

What the satellite saw

Independent evidence of what a disaster did to industrial sites. Radar backscatter from Sentinel-1 and NISAR and night-time lights from VIIRS feed a deterministic engine, and every report is canonically hashed so anyone can check it later.

U5RF-LM02

Language-model research

Models that check instead of remember

Do small language models that answer from a knowledge base follow an edit, keep a deleted fact deleted, and reject a corrupted record? Pre-registered hypotheses and LoRA fine-tunes of 0.5B and 1.5B models, run across Kaggle, an A100 and Kubernetes jobs.

Stack
PyTorch · LoRA · Qwen2.5 · Kubernetes

Explanations that read the room

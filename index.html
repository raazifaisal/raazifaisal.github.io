L4 → L1 · Lowering

All the way down

Most of that work rests on one small loop: a similarity score. Here it is compiled on an M4, one layer at a time.

  1. L4SourceMultiply two vectors element by element and add it all up.
  2. L3IRClang turns it into LLVM IR in SSA form. The loop becomes φ-nodes and one fused multiply-add.
  3. L2AssemblyThe backend picks five AArch64 instructions for the loop body: two loads, one fmadd, a count and a branch.
  4. L1Machine codeEach instruction becomes a 32-bit word. The loop is 20 bytes. This is the multiply-add, bit by bit.
